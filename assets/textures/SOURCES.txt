--- generated/ (June 2026 texture & objects pass) ---
Everything under generated/ is PROCEDURAL — built by scripts/ac/texgen.py from seeded numpy
noise + a bitmap sign font; no external assets. Reproducible from track.config.json
(environment.seed); street-blade signs pull real road names from route.roads. The legacy JPGs
above are superseded by the generated suite (pbr.py now references generated/ only).
Normal maps are DirectX convention (green = down).
